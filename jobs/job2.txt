About the job
Job Description

Join the Legal Tech Revolution at Litera

Are you ready to shape the future of how law is practiced? At Litera, we’re on a mission to Raise The Bar™️ for the legal profession by delivering transformational and globally trusted solutions to law firms and corporate legal teams worldwide. We’ve been a leader in legal tech innovation for 30 years and are leading the legal AI revolution to this day with most of the world’s largest law firms as our clients. If you’re passionate about building AI-forward solutions that scale globally and want your work to impact millions of legal professionals worldwide, this is your opportunity to be part of an extraordinary team that’s elevating the craft of law.

As part of our strategic growth and commitment to fostering collaboration and operational excellence, we are transitioning to a hybrid working model. This position can be based in Toronto and candidates should reside within reasonable commuting distance, as this role requires on-site presence at least three days per week. This hybrid approach enables us to build a collaborative and innovative work environment while maintaining the flexibility that supports both productivity and work-life balance. We are actively seeking talented individuals to join our team in this exciting new phase of growth.

This Product Manager role sits at the heart of Litera’s strategy to modernize how contracts are drafted, negotiated, and finalized.

Role Overview

As a Product Manager focused on Document Comparison, you will drive the evolution of Litera’s comparison experience by transforming the user experience from an isolated desktop application to a compare anywhere experience that connects users into the broader Litera One cloud ecosystem. You will be accountable for meaningful business outcomes, including adoption, retention and revenue impact.

Document comparison is used by nearly every lawyer to generate redlines and understand changes across documents. Your role is to ensure Litera continues to lead this category while moving customers to modern, cloud‑enabled workflows—without compromising the speed, reliability, and trust that high‑frequency users depend on.

Your success will be measured by:

Growth in adoption of cloud‑based comparison experiences 
Sustained usage and retention of the desktop experience 
Successful migration of customers off legacy and end‑of‑life comparison solutions 
Maintained market leadership against direct redlining and comparison competitors 

This role requires deep customer empathy, strong execution instincts, and a relentless focus on outcomes.

Business Outcomes You Will Drive

In this role, you will be directly accountable for: 
Increased adoption of cloud‑based comparison capabilities, driven through enhancements to the desktop experience 
High usage and retention of the desktop comparison workflow during the transition to cloud‑powered architecture 
Successful customer migration from legacy and end‑of‑life comparison tools to modern Litera solutions 
Reduction in customer friction and migration risk through clear parity mapping and targeted enhancements 
Sustained and strengthened market leadership in document comparison and redlining 

Core Product Focus

You will lead strategy and execution for Litera’s document comparison portfolio, including:

Modernizing a desktop comparison application to be cloud‑powered while preserving performance, reliability, and trust 
Creating seamless pathways that guide users from desktop comparison into cloud‑based experiences 
Identifying and closing parity gaps between legacy comparison tools and modern Litera offerings 
Ensuring comparison workflows continue to meet the nuanced, high‑stakes needs of legal professionals 

AI is an important lever in this role, applied thoughtfully to improve accuracy, performance, and confidence, without introducing unpredictability into a workflow lawyers rely on daily. You will across the full product management stack ensuring deep understanding of:

The Customer – Clearly articulating who our customers are and the problems they face in their workflow. You’ll engage directly with customers to learn about their needs and concerns. You’ll present and gather feedback on the roadmap. You’re skilled at synthesizing, understanding, and tracking customer data and use it to help prioritize engineering efforts.

The Competition – Be the expert on what the competition is doing as well as where the market is heading from a trends perspective. You’ll be responsible for a deep understanding of how we differentiate, but not just for difference's sake, but to support our position in the market.

The Requirements – You’ll define engineering efforts by authoring Epics and Stories that clearly define WHO the feature or experience is for, written in the user voice and WHY this effort is valuable. Clear prioritization is critical with a rolling monthly updated roadmap modified and refreshed based on the latest data and customer information.

The Story, The Vision – Everyone from engineering, QA, user experience, marketing and customer facing teams need to know what story we’re trying to tell with the work we’re doing. Not the “What” but the “Why” it matters. A coherent vision told as a story in the user voice is critical for aligning all disciplines to the same goal.

The Roadmap – What is coming next? When is it coming? You’ll drive the beating heart of the Product Development organization. You’ll adjust the prioritization based on customer data, engineering team status and latest strategic priorities. You’ll balance short term tactical delivery-focused monthly releases with longer term strategic approaches to winning in the market. This role will have a specific focus on Kira’s path forward as AI technologies continue to advance.

The User Experience – You’ll partner with the Litera Design team to ensure the user experience is a first-class citizen in our product development efforts. While the Product Manager does not own the design direction in isolation, they must be passionate about the end user experience and advocate for the various personas.

The End-To-End – Your job is to shepherd the development of the product and to enable the Go-To-Market teams so they’re armed to tell the right story and can support our customers with confidence.

Key Responsibilities

Product Strategy & Commercial Impact

Define and own the product vision and roadmap for document comparison 
Drive cloud adoption through enhancements to the desktop experience 
Make prioritization decisions based on adoption, migration success, and competitive impact 

Adoption, Migration & Retention

Treat migration off end‑of‑life tools as a core product outcome 
Identify parity gaps and lead efforts to close them through targeted enhancements 
Partner with Sales and Customer Success to understand blockers to adoption and churn risks 

Platform & AI‑Enabled Modernization

Lead the transition from desktop‑only comparison to a cloud‑powered architecture 
Apply AI thoughtfully to improve accuracy, performance, and usability 
Ensure reliability, trust, and predictability remain non‑negotiable standards 

Competitive Leadership

Maintain deep expertise in the document comparison market 
Drive differentiation that reinforces Litera’s leadership in redlining 
Anticipate competitive moves and adjust strategy proactively 

Cross‑Functional Leadership

Partner closely with engineering, design, and domain experts 
Communicate strategy, trade‑offs, and progress clearly to executive stakeholders 
Champion an outcome‑driven product culture focused on adoption and leadership 

Qualifications

3+ years of product management experience owning customer‑facing workflows 
Proven ability to drive adoption and retention in high‑usage, mission‑critical products 
Experience modernizing legacy or desktop software and introducing cloud capabilities 
Strong customer research and problem‑discovery skills 
Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities 
Experience in legal technology or document‑centric workflows is highly desirable
