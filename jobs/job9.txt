About the job
From our first lot in Cambridge, Ontario, to residential, commercial, and recreational developments in 18 cities across North America, Great Gulf Group has grown into a diversified real estate organization with over 45 years of experience.

While our portfolio has evolved, our commitment remains the same: building great spaces that inspire the people who live, work, and play in them. Today, our multi-faceted business spans commercial, mixed-use and residential development, construction management, software, engineering, panelized manufacturing, architecture, and hospitality.

All companies under the Great Gulf Group umbrella are united by one simple goal: the pursuit of greatness. It’s achieved every day through the passion and dedication of our people. If you’re excited to build meaningful solutions and make an impact, we’d love to hear from you!

Great Gulf is seeking a talented and experienced AI Product Owner to join our team in Toronto.

This role is an integral part of our enterprise AI transformation, focused on identifying and delivering practical AI use cases that improve productivity and drive measurable operational value across the business.

You’ll work closely with business units, the AI implementation team, and the Senior AI Product Owner to move initiatives from idea to pilot to production. This position is ideal for a product professional who is eager to deepen their expertise in applied AI and build a strong foundation in AI-enabled product ownership.

Key Responsibilities

AI Strategy & Use Case Development

Partner with business units to identify, document, and assess AI use cases aligned with productivity gains and operational improvement
Support prioritization of AI initiatives based on business value, feasibility, risk, and organizational readiness
Translate business problems into clear AI-focused requirements, workflows, and success criteria with guidance from senior team members
Contribute to AI initiatives through defined phases—from pilots and prototypes to production deployment

Stakeholder Engagement & Enablement

Act as a bridge between business stakeholders, IT, product, and AI development teams
Facilitate or support workshops, discovery sessions, and working groups to surface opportunities and adoption risks
Support business unit champions and contribute to AI Council activities by preparing materials, insights, and progress updates
Communicate AI concepts, limitations, and outcomes clearly to non-technical audiences

Delivery, Measurement & Governance

Support planning and execution of AI pilots, ensuring clear objectives, evaluation criteria, and graduation paths
Track and report on adoption, usage, and success metrics such as time saved, error reduction, and ROI
Assist with User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and validation of AI-enabled workflows
Ensure AI solutions align with governance standards, acceptable-use policies, data-access rules, and security requirements
Document learnings, best practices, and reusable patterns to support broader adoption

Continuous Improvement

Identify process changes required to fully realize AI benefits beyond tool adoption
Monitor risks such as prompt degradation, vendor changes, shadow AI usage, or stalled adoption
Assess AI tools and models to ensure ongoing relevance and performance, flagging recommendations for review
Support training initiatives, internal communications, and enablement materials related to AI adoption

Qualifications

3+ years of experience as a Product Owner, Product Analyst, or similar role
Experience working with business stakeholders to define and deliver technology-enabled solutions
Demonstrated ability to analyze processes and identify improvement opportunities
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including facilitation and presentations
Strong documentation skills, including requirements, process flows, and reports
Some experience with AI, automation, analytics, or advanced digital tools
Ability to critically evaluate AI-generated outputs for accuracy, relevance, and business fit

Preferred Qualifications

Familiarity with prompt engineering and AI-enabled workflow design
Comfort working in emerging domains where requirements are not fully defined
Hands-on experience with SQL, no-code automation tools, or scripting
Experience in the home builder industry
Proactive in keeping pace with emerging AI trends and translating that into practical recommendations

Physical Requirements

Office environment – sitting/working at desk/computer in a shared office space and/or remote working from home
